# Microbiome Friendly brand/design brief

Use when Aram asks for UX, visual identity, packaging hierarchy, design system, or Claude Design prompts for **Microbiome Friendly**.

## Project identity

Microbiome Friendly is a separate new project, **not Das Experten**. Do not import Das Experten aesthetics, product assumptions, oral-care logic, SKU rules, packaging references, or marketplace tone unless Aram explicitly asks for a bridge.

## Direction

Position as a premium **warm biotech ecosystem brand** around microbiome-friendly products, habits, and formulas. It should feel science-first, human, friendly, and confident — not clinical, not pharmacy-cold, not generic wellness, and not a standard supplement mockup.

Useful strategic references by role:
- Seed: premium microbiome ritual and scientific cleanliness.
- ZOE: ecosystem UX, quiz, app/test/podcast/science/stories layers.
- Viome: diagnostic funnel, data to recommendation to personalized plan.
- Better Dairy: dense confident typography and human biotech warmth.
- Ginkgo Bioworks: macro-bio storytelling and living-systems ambition.
- SNIPR Biome: accessible bacteria/CRISPR science storytelling.

## Aram typography preference

Aram dislikes thin typography. For Microbiome Friendly, do not use thin, hairline, ultra-light, or fragile startup-wellness type as the main style.

Recommended weights:
- Headlines: 600–800.
- Subheads: 500–700.
- Body: 450–550 or visually equivalent.
- Technical labels and science captions: 500–600.

Friendly should mean approachable, not weak. Use dense, readable typography with calm confidence.

## Packaging/system lessons

The current concept uses navy/indigo supplement bottles, white type, a colorful microbiome organism circle, product formula names, benefit taglines, icons, and capsule/dietary info. Main risk: **Microbiome Friendly can read as a small certification badge while product names dominate**.

When briefing or designing, solve brand architecture explicitly:
- A: Microbiome Friendly as master brand.
- B: Microbiome Friendly as certification/standard badge.
- C: hybrid master brand plus formula system.

Preferred default: hybrid master brand plus formula system, unless Aram chooses otherwise.

Product/formula system observed:
- AkkerMagic.
- Neurobiome Reset.
- Skinbiotic.
- Lipobiotic.
- Mentabiotic.
- Enerbiotic.
- Anti-Age Biome.

Anti-Age Biome is the weakest naming direction; prefer modern longevity/vitality language such as Vitalbiome, Longevibiotic, Agebiotic, Regenbiotic, or a better coined option after checking positioning.

## Visual system rules

Use deep navy/indigo as core scientific color, warm ivory/off-white as human background, science white for contrast, and adult controlled microbiome accent colors. Avoid childish rainbow microbiome graphics.

The microbiome organism mark should become proprietary: 5–7 recurring organism shapes, color and mono versions, small-size legibility, favicon/emboss compatibility, and no stock-bacteria look.

Use product accent coding while keeping the family unified:
- Mentabiotic: lavender / calm blue.
- Enerbiotic: amber / electric lime.
- Skinbiotic: coral / rose / skin warmth.
- Lipobiotic: teal / metabolic green.
- AkkerMagic: deep green / gold.
- Neurobiome Reset: indigo / violet.
- Longevity/vitality formula: warm gold / sage.

## UX structure default

Treat the website as an ecosystem brand, not one bottle:
hero brand promise; what microbiome friendly means; product formulas; personal quiz; science layer; how it works; ingredients/mechanisms; stories/outcomes; journal/education; FAQ; shop/subscribe; optional certification/standard explanation.

For conversion work on microbiomefriendly.me, also load `references/microbiome-friendly-conversion-audit.md`. It captures the preferred pre-launch funnel, conversion gate, segment check, legal-link checks, and CTA hierarchy discovered during live site review.

Default conversion path: hero → segment choice → quiz → personalized formula result → email capture → launch list / early access → science proof → preorder/shop when ready.

If checkout is not live, do not make Shop the dominant CTA. Use Join launch, Select formulas, Save my formula plan, or Get early access instead.

Use two-layer benefit language:
- Human benefit first: simple outcome.
- Science mechanism second: support/balance language.

Avoid medical overclaims such as cure, treat, prevent disease. Prefer supports, helps maintain, balance, natural processes, gut-brain axis support, gut-skin axis support, metabolic pathway support.

## Do not do

Do not use Das Experten visual language by default. Do not use thin type, generic supplement layouts, stock bacteria illustrations, hospital green, low-contrast beige wellness, emoji-like icons, unreadable tiny labels, or overpromising medical claims.
