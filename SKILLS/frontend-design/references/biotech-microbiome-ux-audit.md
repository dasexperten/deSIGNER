# Biotech / microbiome / bacteria UX audit notes

Use when Aram asks for beautiful or unusual UX references around microbiome, macrobiome, bacteria, biofilm-adjacent science, precision nutrition, CRISPR-bacteria, synthetic biology, or science-led wellness.

## Audit lens

Do not deliver a bare link dump. Rank sites by design value and annotate:

- **UX mechanism**: macro narrative, diagnostic funnel, quiz, science storytelling, platform/pipeline, ingredient-brand, premium ritual, practitioner/patient split, AI/data interface.
- **Typography**: identify font families and weights when available; flag thin/light weights as a risk for Aram.
- **Borrow / avoid**: translate each reference into an actionable Das Experten design move.
- **Scientific tone**: separate serious biotech explanation from wellness fluff.

## Typography preference

Aram dislikes thin fonts. In this class of task, explicitly call out `100`, `200`, `300`, `light`, and overly delicate serif/display usage. Prefer strong headings and body text around `500–800`, or typefaces that remain optically dense at `400`.

## Reference seed bank

**Ginkgo Bioworks** — https://www.ginkgo.bio/
Best for macro-UX: sells a future-world narrative around autonomous labs and biotechnology infrastructure. Typography observed: Recoleta headings around 400 with strong optical weight; body sans around 400. Borrow the big scientific manifesto opening.

**Better Dairy** — https://betterdairy.com/
Best for strong typography and warm biotech. Precision fermentation explained through human-first copy. Typography observed: Superior Title around 700, TT Commons Pro Mono around 500. Very compatible with Aram’s dislike of thin fonts.

**SNIPR Biome** — https://www.sniprbiome.com/
Best for bacteria / CRISPR storytelling. Uses dramatic problem-to-solution structure and a How It Works section. Typography observed: Syne 700 headings, sans body around 400. Strong reference for explaining complex mechanisms in steps.

## Das Experten translation pattern

A strong Das Experten oral-care application is: human macro-claim → scientific oral-care mechanism → diagnostic/ritual UX → dense confident typography.

Example structure: hero manifesto about oral ecosystem balance; quiz for coffee/tea/smoking/sensitivity/plaque/gums/whitening; science labels for pH, enamel, plaque, microbiome, remineralization; product recommendations; testimonials and proof blocks. Keep it premium-scientific, not pharmacy-generic and not fragile wellness minimalism.
