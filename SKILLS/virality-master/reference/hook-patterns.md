# Hook Patterns — Library by Category

Reference library of hook archetypes that consistently score well, organized by Das Experten product category.

## For premium dental products (ETALON, GROSSE, INNOWEISS premium)

### Pattern: "Result reveal"
First 0-1s: mirror shot, neutral expression
Hook moment 1.5s: smile reveal, white teeth
Score expectation: hook 7-8, retention high

### Pattern: "Side-by-side comparison"
First 1s: split-screen, before/after
Hook moment: caliper or measurement appears
Score expectation: hook 6-7, audience response 8 (saves)

### Pattern: "Expert authority"
First 1s: white coat, clinical setting
Hook moment 1.5s: hands holding product close-up
Best avatar: MrsWaltz, Pevtsova
Score expectation: hook 7, creative 75+

## For wellness/natural products (GINGER FORCE, DETOX, SCHWARZ)

### Pattern: "Ingredient drop"
First 0.5s: empty white surface
Hook moment 1s: fresh ginger root / charcoal / herbs falls into frame
Score expectation: hook 8, distraction risk low

### Pattern: "Morning ritual"
First 1s: window with light, hand on counter
Hook moment 1.5s: tube uncapped, paste squeezed
Best avatar: Helga, Bodrova, Marianna variants
Score expectation: hook 7-8

### Pattern: "Nature parallel"
First 0-1s: forest/leaves/water close-up
Hook moment 1.5s: product enters frame from natural background
Score expectation: hook 8, audience response high (shares)

## For mass-market (DETOX, INNOWEISS base, SYMBIOS)

### Pattern: "Speak directly to camera"
First 0-1s: face centered, slight forward lean
Hook moment 1.5s: spoken word + product appears next to face
Best avatars: Mironova, Marianna, Aura
Score expectation: hook 6-7, depends heavily on facial energy

### Pattern: "Surprise demonstration"
First 1s: ordinary morning scene
Hook moment 1.5s: brushing teeth, foam (visual interest)
Score expectation: hook 7

### Pattern: "Problem-solution"
First 1s: dirty teeth / yellowing CGI / gum bleeding stylized
Hook moment 1.5s: product enters, fast cut to clean result
Score expectation: hook 8, but distraction risk medium (negative imagery)

## For kids products (DE208 EVOLUTION KIDS)

First 0-1s: kid face, mid-smile
Hook moment 1.5s: kid grabs product, makes a face
Score expectation: hook 8 (kids = retention)

## Universal hook anti-patterns (AVOID)

These patterns consistently score below 5:

❌ Slow zoom on static product packshot (no human, no motion)
❌ Wide establishing shot of room/scene (eye doesn't know where to land)
❌ Text-only first frame (people scroll past walls of text on TikTok)
❌ Logo prominent in first 1s (eye treats it as ad, scrolls)
❌ Voice-over starts before visual ("Hi guys" → eye not engaged yet)
❌ Multiple products in first 1s (split attention, weak hook)
❌ Background music intro before content (people listen for content first)
❌ Slow camera dolly (motion is too gradual to register as hook)

## Selection logic for sales-hunter / das-presenter

When integrating, hook choice depends on:

1. **Target market trust level** — high-trust market (DE, UK) tolerates softer hooks; low-trust market (SEA, MENA new entries) needs stronger hooks
2. **Platform** — TikTok needs stronger hooks than Reels; marketplace listing videos can use slower reveal
3. **Funnel stage** — top-of-funnel needs strong pattern interrupt; retargeting can be softer
4. **Avatar archetype** — premium avatars (MrsWaltz, Pevtsova) waste their dignity on aggressive hooks; use slower reveal patterns

For ambiguous case: default to "Camera Bump + Bathroom + Product Review preset" — universal middle-ground that scores 65-70 consistently.
